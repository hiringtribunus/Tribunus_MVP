Tribunus

Data Engine

Tribunus turns a proposed development into an evidence-backed municipal approval strategy.

A developer enters a site and the development they want to build. Tribunus establishes the verified municipal baseline, identifies where the proposal departs from it, uses policy and actual decision history to assess what can credibly be pushed, and produces the most defensible pathway toward approval.

Developers create value by reducing uncertainty. Tribunus reduces the uncertainty between what they want to build and what the municipality may actually approve.

A departure is any gap between what the team proposes and what the rules require. Each one opens as a ticket that carries its own research, comparables, owner and decision.

Where departures come from

AutomaticOn reviewRun Development Review compares the confirmed proposal to the baseline and raises every gap it finds.
EmergentDuring researchStudying precedent or council history surfaces a constraint nobody had modelled.
ManualBy the teamRaised from experience, a consultant flag, or something the City said in a meeting.

Market

The gap

Proven companies sell individual parts of the workflow. The least-proven section is the intelligence between early feasibility and municipal approval — what can be pushed, whether policy and precedent support it, and how to pursue approval.
