Tribunus MVP — Feature Blocks

Version 1.0 · July 2026 · Working draft for team review

P1 — core to first paid pilot P2 — fast follow P3 — later phase
01

Platform and foundation

P1
Company, users & permissions

Org accounts, role-based access, and project teams — developers, analysts, and consultants each see their own slice.

P1
Project workspace

Create a project by address or drawn parcel. Stage, asset type, assumptions, and status all live in one place.

P1
File intake & versioning

Upload PDFs, spreadsheets, correspondence, and drawings with preserved versions and full audit history.

P1
Security & admin controls

Encryption, logging, retention policy, source management, user support, and pilot analytics.

02

Municipal data core

The always-current layer the developer cared about most.

P1
Vancouver source registry

Authoritative zoning, plans, policies, fees, GIS, application, council, and permit sources. The trust backbone.

P1
Time-versioned rules & fees

Current and historical requirements with effective dates and source links. The always-updated DCC, DCL, and CAC feed.

P1
Deterministic calculators

FSR, density, unit counts, dedications, and fees from approved formulas — never free-form model math.

P2
Zoning map aggregation

Every municipality a user works in, in one place, instead of ten city maps open across one screen.

03

Project setup and verification

P1
Project profile extraction

Pull height, density, units, tenure, parking, setbacks, floor area, uses, and departures — then confirm key facts with the user.

P1
Verified Project Baseline

One view of current zoning, plans, policies, fees, approval path, site constraints, and effective dates.

P1
Assumption Ledger

Every material assumption: source, last checked, confidence, and what it costs if it turns out wrong.

P1
Verification & citations

Original-source citations, confidence labels, contradictory-evidence checks, and a human-review queue. Fact over summary.

P3
Client standards absorption

Pre-load a firm's own historical numbers and standards so their data is ready to drop in without repeat conversations.

04

Analysis features

P1
Feature-run engine

One-click analyses — Baseline Review, Fee Verification, Precedent Review, Amendment Risk, Application Readiness — each a saved, verified job.

P1
Automatic Assumption Audit

Fires on project creation: baseline, risks, precedents, actions. Value arrives before the user learns any features.

05

Precedent and council intelligence

P1
Precedent search & decision trace

Comparable applications reconstructed end to end: original proposal, staff concerns, revisions, council discussion, conditions, outcome.

P2
Gray-area departure precedent

Where a council or member has actually allowed a departure — a 2.5m setback where 3m is required — within the NCP or a 500m radius.

P2
Live council summarization

The gist of a specific council item, and what council actually debated versus what the written OCP or NCP says. No 3-hour meetings.

P2
Council voting profile

Per-member read of how each has voted on comparable projects and the themes they back or resist, each point cited to a meeting.

06

Consultant coordination

The real pain once research is handled. Scoping is an open team question.

P2/P3
Requirement & dependency checklist

Per-discipline and utility checks confirming each package was reviewed and is "in." Mispiped drawings once cost $100k.

P2/P3
Approval sequence map

The required order of approvals — city, then BC Hydro, then utilities — and who governs each decision, with the lag each adds.

07

Monitoring and retention

What keeps veteran users engaged across multi-year project cycles.

P1
Project Watch

Project-specific alerts on policy, fee, infrastructure, and nearby-development changes — with stated impact, not generic news.

P1
Change & upgrade alerts

Any change from City Hall or senior government affecting the property: fees, tax, regulation. The single MVP pick.

P2
Issue backlog log

A running log of past issues tied to the project so they resurface later instead of being forgotten.

P2
Competition alerts

Flags similar product filed nearby, or 500 condo units two blocks away completing just after yours. Filterable by market.

P3
Competitor extras analysis

What nearby competitors offer — second fridge, AC, wood floors, basement suite — so you know the minimum competitive standard.

08

Market and site intelligence

P3
Growth forecasting

GVRD growth strategy, municipal OCPs, and transit plans, so users get into an area before land prices climb.

P3
Neighborhood context signals

Visual transit, bus, mall, and amenity context — the "no mall within a 15-minute drive" test that keeps users out of bad deals.

P3
Sales comps & feasibility check

Local land and product sales with dollar-per-square-foot and timeframe, plus cost per unit by product type. Does it pencil?

09

Cost intelligence

P3
Construction cost benchmarking

Cost per square foot by building type across municipalities, plus component costs, as a check that tender numbers are in range.

P3
Building permit fee estimation

Estimate permit fees from cost per square foot so the city's number goes into the pro forma without surprises.

10

Outputs and collaboration

P1
Risk & Action Register

Findings become persistent issues with owners, due dates, status, and resolution evidence.

P1
Exports & reporting

Shareable decision brief, issue register, and source appendix for internal and consultant review.

P2
Ask This Project

Source-cited chat over verified project context, analyses, and documents. Explicitly not a blank chatbot.

Principles, deferrals, and open decisions

Simplicity and stability

Dead simple for a non-technical user, and no gratuitous changes to settings or layouts. Stability over novelty.

Sectioned, role-based views

Clearly delineated sections — zoning, dollar per square foot, sales, finance — so each role focuses and skips the rest.

Deferred out of V1

No permit submission, code certification, construction management, or universal approval-probability score. Crowded or liability-heavy.

Marketplace & advertising

Far future and permissioned only. Zero tolerance for unsolicited spam, so any such feature needs a hard off switch.

Open: consultant scope

Coordination may be the primary time sink once research is solved. Core to the wedge, or fast follow?

Open: who the wedge is for

Audit and baseline land newer firms; monitoring and cost intelligence retain veterans. Tag features by audience.
